# 🏁 13-0: WRC Draft — Can You Sweep the World Rally Championship?

Draft 5 legendary WRC rally drivers across 6 eras and try to win every round of a 13-round World Rally Championship.

## How to Play

1. **Pick 5 drivers** from any era (1973-2026)
2. **Race 13 rounds** across gravel, tarmac, and snow surfaces
3. **Win every round** to achieve the legendary 13-0 sweep

## Features

- **84 rally legends** across 6 eras (Röhrl / McRae / Loeb / Ogier / Rovanperä)
- **13 rounds** on the 2026 WRC calendar (Monte Carlo → Saudi Arabia)
- **3 surface types** with different bonuses:
  - **Gravel** (8 rounds): Maximum chaos, surface ruts evolve
  - **Tarmac** (4 rounds): Cleaner surface, more consistent grip
  - **Snow** (1 round - Sweden): Studded tires, narrow margins
- **Power Stage** (R13 Saudi Arabia): 2x points for the finale
- **Era Synergy**: +1.5 OVR per extra driver from same era (encourage era-themed teams)
